Ports

The server listens on TCP 10900, 10901, 10800 and 10980, and UDP 10800. To host a race, forward UDP 3658 on your router to your own computer and allow it in its firewall: the other players' games connect to it directly.

Then start the game, choose Play Online, create a profile, pick a car and join a room.
