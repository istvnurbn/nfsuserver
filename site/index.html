Start playing

  1. Start the game and choose Play Online.
  2. Create a profile, agree to the rules and click Use Profile.
  3. Log in, or create an account the first time.
  4. Pick a car and join a room. Create a game there, or join one.

Hosting a race

Joining races needs nothing extra. To host one, the other players' games connect straight to your computer, so:

  • forward UDP port 3658 on your router to your computer, and
  • allow that port in your computer's firewall.

Without it you can still race; just let someone else host.

Server ports

For reference, if something doesn't connect. They are open on the server; players don't need to do anything with them.

PortWhat it is for
